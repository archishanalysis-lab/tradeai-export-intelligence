Building AI Infrastructure For Global Trade

TradeAI helps exporters, importers and MSMEs discover buyers, analyze markets and automate international trade decisions with practical intelligence.

Why TradeAI Exists

Global trade should feel searchable, trustworthy and actionable.

Exporters and MSMEs still lose time moving between buyer lists, customs data, manual spreadsheets, supplier directories and disconnected communication channels. TradeAI brings these workflows into one platform so teams can identify promising markets, verify company signals and act on buyer opportunities faster.

Our goal is to make international trade intelligence accessible to growing businesses, not only large enterprises with expensive data tools. The platform combines marketplace identity, product management, inquiry workflows, analytics and AI recommendations into one operating layer for global commerce.
