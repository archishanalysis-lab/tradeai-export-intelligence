Can I start without paying?

Yes. The Free plan is for basic exploration. Serious exporters should upgrade when they need reports, buyer discovery and deeper country intelligence.

Is buyer discovery included in every plan?

Basic buyer source suggestions are included in Pro. Buyer discovery support can be added based on scope.

Is document review legal certification?

No. TradeAI provides AI-assisted document review and checklist guidance. Final compliance should be verified by a qualified professional or relevant authority.

Which plan is best for SMEs?

Pro is best for most SMEs. Growth is better for active exporters, consultants or teams handling multiple products and markets.
