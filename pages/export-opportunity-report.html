This section shows how TradeAI can flag early review areas. It requires expert and human verification before export decisions.

Documentation readiness

Check invoice, packing list, certificate needs and buyer-specific documentation.

HS-code validation

Confirm classification against product composition and destination rules.

Country-specific compliance

Review labeling, food/product standards and restricted category requirements.

Payment/logistics risk

Compare payment terms, freight lane readiness and delivery lead-time risk.

Human review required
