# TradeAI Project Overview

## Product Name

TradeAI

## Product Type

AI-powered export-import intelligence platform for Indian exporters, importers, SMEs, consultants and trade teams.

## Main Objective

TradeAI helps users discover export-import opportunities, compare product-country potential, identify buyer/supplier leads, understand HS-code risks, generate AI-powered trade reports and manage trade workflows.

The platform is not only a website. It is intended to become a practical B2B SaaS and intelligence platform for export-import businesses.

## Phase 1 Target Corridors

TradeAI currently focuses on 9 target markets grouped into 3 corridors:

### India → East Africa

- Kenya
- Tanzania
- Uganda
- Rwanda

Purpose:

- Export opportunity discovery
- Buyer and distributor discovery
- Product-country reports
- HS-code and document readiness guidance

### India → Gulf / GCC

- UAE
- Saudi Arabia
- Oman
- Qatar

Purpose:

- Premium export market discovery
- Buyer/distributor discovery
- Re-export and Gulf market intelligence
- Product-country scoring

### India ↔ China

- China

Purpose:

- Sourcing intelligence
- Supplier comparison
- Import dependency analysis
- Price benchmarking
- Competitor and product-category tracking
